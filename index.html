Where do you stand?

84 statements. Three questions underneath all of them: who should run the economy, how much power the state should have, and whether society should change its values or keep them.

About 12 minutes.

The map

Every ideology in the test, placed on the economic and authority axes. Tap or hover a dot. The third axis, culture, is shown in the results as a separate bar.

The four corners

Authoritarian left

A strong state used to pursue economic equality and collective ownership.

Leninism, Maoism, Titoism, Chavism

Authoritarian right

A strong state used to protect order, hierarchy, nation or faith, with private property kept.

Fascism, absolute monarchy, national conservatism

Libertarian left

Equality and shared ownership reached without a ruling state, through self-management and mutual aid.

Anarcho-communism, syndicalism, council communism

Libertarian right

Free markets and personal freedom, with the state kept small or abolished.

Libertarianism, classical liberalism, anarcho-capitalism
